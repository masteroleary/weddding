Questions

Everything you need to know before the big day

The Celebration
When should I arrive?+
We recommend arriving at least 15–20 minutes early. Please be seated by 5:15 PM — the ceremony begins promptly at 5:30 PM and we'd hate for you to miss a moment!
Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors?+
Both the ceremony and reception will be held at Hotel Colonnade in Coral Gables — a stunning indoor venue filled with beautiful architecture and elegance throughout.
Can I take photos during the ceremony?+
We are having an unplugged ceremony — please keep phones and cameras put away so everyone can be fully present. Our photographer will capture every moment. Photos are very welcome at the reception!
Guests & Logistics
Can I bring my kids?+
Absolutely! Children are welcome to join us in celebrating. We can't wait to share this special day with your whole family!
Can I bring a plus-one?+
Due to venue capacity, we can only accommodate guests named on your invitation. If a plus-one has been reserved for you, it will be noted on your invite. Please reach out directly if you have any questions — we appreciate your understanding!
Is there parking at the venue?+
Yes! Hotel Colonnade offers parking on site. The hotel is also just 3 miles from Miami International Airport, making it easy to grab an Uber, Lyft, or taxi if you'd rather skip the drive.
Food & Gifts
What if I have a dietary restriction?+
We want everyone to eat well! Please indicate any dietary restrictions or allergies when you RSVP. Note them in the dietary field on the form and we'll make sure you're taken care of.
Should I bring a gift to the venue?+
Please do not bring gifts to the venue — we'd love for you to ship them in advance or contribute online through our registry. Your presence is the greatest gift of all!
Staying in Touch
How do I get updates?+
Check back on this website for any updates leading up to the big day. We'll keep everything current here so you're always in the loop!
