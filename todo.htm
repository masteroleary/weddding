Things to Do

Hit the Beach

Coral Gables is minutes from some of Miami's best beaches. Crandon Park Beach on Key Biscayne is a local favorite — calm turquoise water, lush surroundings, and far less crowded than South Beach.

6747 Crandon Blvd, Key Biscayne

photo
🏊

Venetian Pool

One of the most unique pools in the world, the historic Venetian Pool is a stunning 820,000-gallon lagoon carved from coral rock, complete with waterfalls and grottos. A must-see Coral Gables landmark!

2701 De Soto Blvd, Coral Gables
Website Map
photo
🍽️

Miracle Mile Dining

Coral Gables' vibrant Miracle Mile is lined with exceptional restaurants. Don't miss Bulla Gastrobar for Spanish tapas, Seasons 52 for fresh seasonal cuisine, or La Dorada for outstanding seafood.

Miracle Mile, Coral Gables
Website Map
photo
🛍️

Shops at Merrick Park

An open-air luxury shopping destination featuring Neiman Marcus, Nordstrom, and dozens of boutiques and restaurants. Perfect for a relaxed afternoon of browsing and dining al fresco.

358 San Lorenzo Ave, Coral Gables
Website Map
photo
🎨

Wynwood Arts District

Just 20 minutes away, Miami's Wynwood neighborhood is a world-famous outdoor art gallery with massive murals, galleries, breweries, and trendy cafés. Great for an afternoon adventure.

Wynwood Walls · 2516 NW 2nd Ave, Miami
Website Map
photo
🚤

Explore Biscayne Bay

Rent a boat, kayak, or paddleboard and explore the gorgeous Biscayne Bay. Several marinas offer half-day rentals, and the views of the Miami skyline from the water are unforgettable.

Crandon Park Marina · 4000 Crandon Blvd, Key Biscayne
Website Map
photo
🌿

Fairchild Tropical Botanic Garden

A stunning 83-acre tropical garden featuring rare palms, flowering trees, and a butterfly garden. A peaceful escape into Florida's lush natural beauty — just minutes from the wedding venue.

10901 Old Cutler Rd, Coral Gables
Website Map
photo
🍹

Little Havana

Immerse yourself in Miami's vibrant Cuban culture along Calle Ocho. Sip a cafecito, watch a domino game in Domino Park, grab a Cuban sandwich, and soak up the music and energy of this iconic neighborhood.

Domino Park · 801 SW 15th Ave, Miami
